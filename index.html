Plan your crop step by step

Answer a few simple questions before sowing. We will connect your location, soil, crop, water, inputs, costs, and harvest estimate into one recommendation.

8 simple steps

1. Your location

Location makes the weather and irrigation advice local to your field.

Fallback: Ludhiana, Punjab

2. Soil test

Enter values from your lab report, or leave them blank and we will remind you to test before fertilizer decisions.

If you do not have a report, ask a soil lab or local PAU/KVK office. Do not increase fertilizer only because a crop looks pale.

3. Choose a crop

Pick the crop you are considering; the final comparison will still show alternatives.

4. Farm size and water

Tell us how much land you will sow and how the crop can receive water.

5. Fertilizer and pesticides

Choose a safe planning approach. Soil-test-based is preferred; enter your expected per-acre costs.

6. Harvest and selling costs

Include harvesting, drying, bags, transport, storage, and market charges in your plan.

7. Cattle and leftover plan

Tell us whether crop leftovers will feed cattle, be sold, or return to the soil.

The planner estimates leftover quantity, safe usable quantity, cattle-feed days, and possible sale value. Never feed moldy or contaminated residue and never burn it.

8. Check the result

We now combine your inputs with the local weather signal and crop alternatives.
